Privacy Policy

Top Tipster · Last updated: September 2026

This privacy policy describes how Top Tipster (“we”, “the app”) collects, uses, and protects your information. Top Tipster is a fantasy sports application for football and racing. You can create an account, join or organise competitions (including Last Man Standing and Tipster20 football modes, and racing competitions), make selections, and view standings, results, and related features. The app is for entertainment and friendly competition. It does not involve real-money gambling, stakes, or payouts through the app.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address – when you sign up, used to create and secure your account and to sign in.
  • Username – chosen by you at sign-up, shown on leaderboards and to other participants in your competitions.
  • Password – stored securely by our authentication provider; we do not have access to your actual password.

Competition and gameplay data

  • Display name – when you join a competition you may provide a display name used in that competition.
  • Selections and picks – for example football team picks in Last Man Standing, Tipster20 / goalscorer-related gameplay data, and racing selections, so we can score results and show standings.
  • Competition participation – which competitions you have joined or organised, join/rejoin requests, roles (e.g. player, organiser, manager), and related status so we can run those competitions correctly.
  • Club / Gamemaster information – if you use organiser (Gamemaster) features, we store club setup details and related account/entitlement information needed to run clubs and competitions.

Subscriptions and entitlements

  • We store your plan or entitlement status (for example free vs paid tiers, join limits, whether ads are shown, and organiser capabilities). If you purchase a subscription or in-app product through an app store, payment is handled by Apple or Google; we receive confirmation of purchase status as needed to unlock features, not your full payment card details.

Notifications (optional)

  • Local reminders – if you turn on reminders for a competition, preferences may be stored on your device so we can show optional local notifications (e.g. before pick deadlines).
  • Web / push notifications – if you enable push or web push (for example deadline alerts, join-request notices, or competition updates), we store the subscription endpoint and related keys needed to deliver those messages. You can disable these in the app or browser settings.

Quick access (optional)

  • If you use in-app “Quick access”, we store a link between your account and a quick access code so a shared device can display content for your competitions. This data is only used to provide that feature.

Advertising and device identifiers (free / ad-supported use)

  • If your account is on an ad-supported plan, we show advertisements using Google AdMob (and related Google advertising services).
  • This may involve advertising identifiers on your device, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device/app information, and consent/preference signals (including via Google’s User Messaging Platform where required, for example in the EEA/UK).
  • You may see banner ads and, for some screens (for example standings or goalscorers), a short rewarded ad before content unlocks for the session.
  • Paid or staff accounts that are not ad-supported do not receive these ads in the app.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect precise GPS location for the core fantasy features.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. How we use your information

We use your data to operate and improve Top Tipster:

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To run competitions, store selections, calculate outcomes, and show standings and results.
  • To show your username or display name to other participants in the same competition.
  • To support organiser tools (approving joins, managing competitions, club setup, and related admin features).
  • To apply subscription and entitlement rules (join limits, ads, organiser access).
  • To send optional local or push notifications you have enabled.
  • To show advertising on ad-supported accounts, subject to applicable consent requirements.
  • To provide customer support and protect the security and integrity of the service.

We do not sell your personal data. Advertising partners such as Google may process data as described in their own policies when ads are shown.

3. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we typically rely on:

  • Contract – to provide the account and competition features you request.
  • Legitimate interests – to secure the service, prevent abuse, and improve reliability, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent – where required for non-essential cookies/storage, personalized advertising, or similar processing (including via the in-app consent form where shown). You can withdraw consent where applicable through the consent / privacy options presented in the app or device settings.

4. Where your data is stored and who we share it with

Supabase – account and app data (email, username, selections, competition participation, entitlements, push subscription records, etc.) are stored and processed using Supabase for authentication and database hosting. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Google AdMob / Google advertising services – used to serve ads on ad-supported accounts. See Google’s advertising and privacy documentation for details of their processing.

Apple / Google – if you buy a subscription or related product, the relevant app store processes payment and may share purchase status with us so we can unlock features.

Other users – usernames, display names, picks (when revealed by competition rules), and standings are visible to other participants in the same competition as part of normal gameplay.

We do not share your data with third parties for their own marketing, except as needed for advertising partners to serve ads when you are on an ad-supported plan and as required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from within the app (Account → Delete account). When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your account and authentication details
  • Your profile (username)
  • Your competition participations and related gameplay data tied to your account
  • Any quick access codes linked to your account
  • Related join requests and notification subscriptions linked to your account

After deletion, we do not retain copies longer than needed for normal backup and system operation. Aggregated or anonymised competition history that no longer identifies you may remain. You can also sign out without deleting your account; your data remains until you delete the account.

6. Children and age

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 16, you may need a parent or guardian’s consent to use the app in some regions. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data: secure authentication, encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access controls. You are responsible for keeping your sign-in details secure. If you think your account has been compromised, please change your password or contact us.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You can update account details in the app and delete your account via Account → Delete account. For other requests, contact us using the details below. EEA/UK users may also complain to their local data protection authority.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy at the same URL (or in the app) and change the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy. For significant changes, we may notify you in the app or via the contact details you have provided.
